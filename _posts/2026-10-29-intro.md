Starting on the 12th of November, we will be holding introductory talks for the main categories of Capture the Flag.
We meet on Thursdays at 7pm in the CS building [50.34](https://www.kit.edu/campusplan/map.php?id=50.34) **room -101**. Please bring a laptop if possible.

**Attention:** This is not our customary meeting place. We meet there for space reasons.

Please note that the talks **will be held in German** since both our speakers and our audience are predominantly German-speaking.
We will do our best to accommodate English-speaking participants as well (e.g., summarize after the talk, Q&A in English), and of course we'll do our best to support beginners currently learning German.
If this applies to you, please [reach out](mailto:team@kitctf.de) in advance so that we can plan more effectively or clarify any uncertainties.

 * 12.11.26: What are CTFs? & Web Security
 * 19.11.26: Reverse Engineering
 * 26.11.26: Binary Exploitation
 * 03.12.26: Cryptography

Slides will be published here after the talks.
